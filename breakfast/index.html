The morning table

A generous table, in the shadow of history

Five minutes from Hagia Sophia, one street from the Basilica Cistern. Our day opens with a Turkish breakfast spread and warm bread straight from the oven.

Eggs off the pan, menemen, a full board of cheeses, olives, honey with clotted cream, seasonal jams, warm simit and endless brewed tea. Order the full spread to share, or pick your own plate. Everything is written clearly on the menu, with no surprise bill.

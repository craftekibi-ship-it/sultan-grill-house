At the grill

A charcoal grill in the shadow of history

Five minutes from Hagia Sophia, one street from the Basilica Cistern. We mince our kebab meat by hand, sear the steaks over charcoal and buy our fish fresh each morning.

Our kitchen is halal, our service is warm and the bill is exactly as written on the menu. The rest is a simple table, an evening that never feels rushed and the last embers of the grill well past midnight.
